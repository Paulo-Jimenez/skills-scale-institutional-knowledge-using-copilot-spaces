# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Coordinator

### Role Summary
Release Coordinators oversee the end-to-end release planning, coordination, and communication process. They act as the central hub between engineering, product, QA, and stakeholder teams to ensure smooth deployments.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule release timelines with all cross-functional teams
- Coordinate pre-release requirements (QA sign-off, documentation, release notes)
- Manage release communication and stakeholder notifications
- Coordinate with deployment teams to ensure successful production rollouts
- Manage rollback procedures and incident response during deployments
- Track release metrics and post-release validations
- Document lessons learned and release postmortems

### Goals
- Reduce release cycle time and deployment risk
- Ensure clear communication across all teams
- Achieve consistent, predictable release cadences
- Minimize production incidents and unplanned rollbacks

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and coordination calls
- Release notes and stakeholder announcements
- Deployment checklists and status dashboards
- Post-release retrospectives and incident reports

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers on timelines and dependencies
- Collaborates with QA Lead on test readiness and sign-off
- Partners with Developers on deployment readiness
- Reports to Product Managers on release status and customer impact
- Coordinates with Business Analysts on documentation and customer communication

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the quality assurance strategy, test planning, and defect management for projects. They ensure that all acceptance criteria are met and that deployments are safe and reliable.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plans for projects
- Schedule and coordinate QA activities within sprints and releases
- Design and execute test cases covering acceptance criteria
- Manage defect discovery, tracking, and escalation
- Perform regression testing before deployments
- Conduct release readiness assessments and smoke testing
- Report test coverage and quality metrics
- Identify and propose quality improvements

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality deliverables meet acceptance criteria
- Reduce defects in production through thorough testing
- Achieve consistent test coverage targets
- Enable faster release cycles through efficient testing

### Typical Communication
- QA status updates in standups and sprint planning
- Defect reports and quality dashboards
- Release readiness assessments
- Test case documentation and traceability matrices

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborates with Developers on test coverage and acceptance criteria
- Works with Release Coordinator on deployment readiness
- Partners with Business Analysts on requirements validation
- Reports to Project Managers on quality risks and blockers
- Supports Product Managers with quality metrics

---

## Change Control Board Representative

### Role Summary
Change Control Board (CCB) Representatives act as governance liaisons, reviewing and approving significant scope changes, architecture decisions, and risk impacts. They ensure that changes align with business objectives and organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review change requests for scope, timeline, and resource impact
- Assess technical and business risks of proposed changes
- Approve or recommend adjustments to requested changes
- Communicate decisions and rationale to stakeholders
- Track approved changes and ensure adherence to commitments
- Escalate critical changes to senior leadership when needed
- Maintain change logs and audit trails
- Balance business needs with organizational governance

### Goals
- Prevent scope creep while supporting necessary changes
- Ensure changes align with organizational priorities
- Minimize unplanned work and resource conflicts
- Maintain accountability and transparency in change management

### Typical Communication
- Change request reviews and governance meetings
- Decision memos and change approval communications
- Escalation notices to leadership
- Change tracking dashboards and audit reports

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with Project Managers to assess impact on schedules
- Collaborates with Product Managers on business value of changes
- Works with Developers and QA to understand technical implications
- Acts as liaison between project teams and organizational governance
- Reports to stakeholders on approved changes and exceptions

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business needs and technical solutions. They gather, clarify, maintain, and communicate requirements to ensure that delivered features meet business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Clarify ambiguous requirements and resolve conflicts
- Translate business requirements into acceptance criteria
- Maintain requirement traceability and change management
- Validate that delivered features meet business requirements
- Support user acceptance testing (UAT) planning and execution
- Document process improvements and lessons learned
- Act as point of contact for requirement changes and clarifications

### Goals
- Ensure delivered features meet business expectations
- Reduce rework due to misaligned or unclear requirements
- Improve stakeholder satisfaction and adoption
- Enable data-driven decision-making through insights

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and specification documents
- UAT plans and test scripts
- Stakeholder workshops and clarification meetings
- Change request reviews and impact assessments

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers on feature prioritization
- Works with Project Managers on requirement change impacts
- Partners with Developers on acceptance criteria and technical feasibility
- Supports QA Lead on test case design and coverage
- Engages Release Coordinator on documentation needs
- Communicates with stakeholders on requirement status and changes

---

## Customer Success Partner

### Role Summary
Customer Success Partners represent customer needs, feedback, and outcomes within the project. They ensure that features deliver real customer value and that go-to-market strategy aligns with customer expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer feedback and pain points
- Represent customer perspective in design and prioritization discussions
- Collect early feedback through beta testing and pilots
- Document customer use cases and success criteria
- Communicate feature updates and changes to customers
- Coordinate customer training and adoption support
- Monitor customer satisfaction and adoption metrics
- Identify expansion opportunities based on customer feedback

### Goals
- Ensure features deliver tangible customer value
- Maximize customer adoption and satisfaction
- Reduce time to customer value and support issues
- Build strong customer relationships and loyalty

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reports and voice-of-customer insights
- Customer beta testing and pilot coordination
- Customer release notes and training materials
- Adoption metrics and customer satisfaction reports

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on customer prioritization
- Collaborates with Business Analysts on requirement validation
- Supports Project Managers on customer communication timelines
- Works with QA Lead on customer acceptance testing
- Coordinates with Release Coordinator on customer launch planning
- Engages with Developers on feature usability and customer scenarios

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- New personas (Release Coordinator, QA Lead, CCB Representative, Business Analyst, Customer Success Partner) extend the core team to include critical governance, quality, and customer-centric perspectives.
